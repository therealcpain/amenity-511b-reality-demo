Disclaimer. This card is cause → amenity literacy — not legal advice, not a claim filer, and not an airline customer-service tool. Amenity commitments (meals / hotels) are airline-specific and hinge on whether the disruption is coded as Air Carrier controllable. DOT final rule (FR Sep 3 2026 / 2026-18040) creates a Section 511(b) reporting category for ten statutory events excluded from “Air Carrier,” effective October 19, 2026. Refund rights for cancel / significant change are separate — see flightrights.gov · transportation.gov refunds · Gate Rights Card (refund JTBD — not cloned here). We never invent the airline’s official BTS code or a $ hotel/meal amount. Sources: FR Sep 3 2026 (2026-18040) · Crowell Sep 3 · View from the Wing Sep 4 · Simple Flying Sep 5.

How to read the card

  • Amenity-likely Travel/view date ≥ Oct 19 2026 and cause is still other Air Carrier controllable (not one of the ten 511(b) events). Meals/hotels may still apply under airline customer-service plans — commitments are airline-specific.
  • Amenity-unlikely Date ≥ Oct 19 and cause is in the ten 511(b) events, or already framed as weather / NAS / security / late aircraft. Overnight hotel is especially unlikely under 511(b) framing.
  • Pre-rule Date < Oct 19 2026 — rule not yet effective. Card still shows which amenity bucket the cause would map to after Oct 19.
  • Unsure Cause chip = unsure — we will not invent a BTS code or amenity outcome.
  • Under-hero status flag restates Pre-rule countdown / Amenity-likely / Amenity-unlikely / Unsure in text (not color-only).
  • Refunds ≠ amenities. Ticket refund rights for cancel / significant change are separate. Pointers: flightrights.gov · transportation.gov · Gate Rights Card. This card does not clone refund logic.
  • No invented $ or BTS codes. Empty/missing date = honest miss.
